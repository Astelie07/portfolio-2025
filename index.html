About

Astélie HENRYON

Currently pursuing a Master's degree in Game Design, I am looking for a final-year internship where I can apply and further develop my skills while contributing to meaningful game projects.

I am a young French artist who has been passionate about video games, drawing, and many other forms of art since childhood. These passions have inspired me throughout my life, and today I hope to inspire others in return. For over two years, I have been working with my sister on a game project that is, unfortunately, still unnamed. Alongside this project, I continue filling notebooks with ideas for characters, stories, and enemies that I find interesting.

I have far more ideas than I can realistically work on, which is why I currently have several notebooks filled with concepts for different games. One of them is about to enter active development in the coming weeks.

While I may appear to have a clear vision of what I want to achieve, I have also developed a versatile skill set that allows me to contribute to many different aspects of game creation, from creative writing to artistic and technical tasks.

Endheld

You’ve just found a lost console, what will you do with it?

· Informations

Type : Point and Click / Puzzle

Plateform : PC

Status : Available

Credit : Game & Narrative Designer / Assist Programmer

🎮 Page itch.io

✨ Pitch

Endheld is a narrative interface exploration game inspired by Nintendo’s 2010s aesthetic. Find clues in conversations, records and pictures to delve into the world of Luca: a queer teenager who used his handheld console as a refuge from the family and identity-related challenges he faced. Between melancholy and nostalgia, his story is now in your hands.
Note: The game was originally designed to be played on a Steam Deck to provide a more immersive experience. It was later adapted for PC.

✨ Gameplay

🌍

Exploration

🔍

🫧
